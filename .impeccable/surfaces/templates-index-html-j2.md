Scope: the daily game (home page, `/`), mode Operate. Audience: Ooze players and alumni on phones from the group chat. Task: name the hidden player in six guesses from arrow-only feedback, then share. Constraints: arrows only (no numbers in guess cells), one daily puzzle from any season with a season filter, not busy or gimmicky, keyboard playable, feedback never by color alone.

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/comp-1.png

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today's puzzle is posted like the day's schedule zip-tied to the tournament fence: a giant field-style number, a pool label, ruled bracket lines. It refuses the Wordle tile grid and the stats-dashboard card stack.
OWN-WORLD: White board stock #f6f5f0, ink #141414, one marker orange #ff5a1f (Guess button, current row number), hairline rules #d9d7cf, heavy black rules. One heavy condensed grotesk for every number and head; heavy arrow glyphs; equals as a solid ink block with a white "="; wrong guesses struck through, never removed. Flat: no shadows, gradients or textures.
STORY: The player sees which tournament the line is from, guesses teammates, reads arrows, narrows down, wins or loses, shares the result, and taps through to the player's page.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim bar (OOZEDLE wordmark left; Players, Tournaments right). Header: "#214" huge at left, a vertical rule, then date, "PDQ · 2026" large, "Name the player. 6 guesses." Six-row ruled table (# · Player · PP A G B T) filling the middle. Input "Guess a player" plus orange Guess button directly below the table. "Yesterday" section starts at the bottom edge.
